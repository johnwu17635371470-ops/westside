Compare the right measurement before ordering

Compare the same measurement for the exact variant you plan to order. Note the unit and what was measured: a product dimension does not automatically establish body size or fit. Use these checks to identify missing details before choosing.

Measurements to compare by jewelry type
Selection guideCompareConfirm before ordering
Rings The sizing system and each ring's size. Request numerical adjustment limits; an adjustable label alone does not establish fit.
Necklaces Base length, extension and hanging drop separately. Check the selected chain combination and included pieces.
Bracelets & arm cuffs Inner diameter, product length and body circumference separately. Confirm the wearing position and adjustment limits; these measurements are not interchangeable.
Hoop earrings Inner diameter versus overall outer size. Check thickness and whether weight describes one earring, one pair or the complete set.

If a needed dimension is missing or conflicting, confirm it for the selected variant before ordering. Attributed material descriptions and AI-styled photos do not independently verify composition, measurements or package contents. See Shipping & Returns for ordering policies.
